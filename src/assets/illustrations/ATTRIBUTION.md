## Gokyo throws (40 PNGs)

The 40 PNG illustrations for the throws were extracted from the **Gokyo-no-waza**
composite poster on Wikimedia Commons:

- Source: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Gokyo-no-waza.jpg>
- Author: Wikimedia user "Mtwist" (signed M2012 / M2013 in the original drawings)
- License: see the source page on Wikimedia Commons (typically Creative Commons —
  verify the current license before redistributing)

Each cell of the original poster has been individually cropped, rescaled to 600px
wide, and saved keyed by the technique slug used in this app. The cropping
preserves the composition; no figures were modified.

## Replacing or adding illustrations

If you replace any of these with your own animated GIFs or with another source,
update or remove the entry above. The frontend cascade tries
`<slug>.{gif,webp,svg,png,jpg}` in order, then the remote `image_url`, then the
category silhouette in `../silhouettes/`.
